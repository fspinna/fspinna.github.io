## Lectures

The full content of the course is available on the official Teams Channel after approval.

The notebooks expect the datasets in a `Data/` folder next to them: [download data.zip](/assets/courses/artificial-intelligence-ii/notebooks/data.zip).

Two datasets are not included in the archive because of their size, and need to be placed in `Data/` separately: `dermamnist.npz` (used in the CNN notebook, available through the [MedMNIST](https://medmnist.com/) package) and `promoter_nonpromoter.csv` (used in the linear models notebook).
